What is Quarterly Compliance?

Set the compliance percentage each engineer receives of their quarterly bonus. 100% = full bonus, 90% = 90% of the tier amount, 80% = 80% of the tier amount.

Select the quarter and year, choose a percentage per engineer, then click Save Compliance Data. Engineers will see their adjusted bonus figure in the portal.

100% — Full bonus
90% — 90% of bonus
80% — 80% of bonus
